hook: does the first moment make a scrolling viewer stop?
  1 = opens on setup, small talk or scenery; nothing happens in the first lines
  2 = opens on mild tension or a line that only matters if you know the story

cliffhanger: does it end unresolved so the viewer needs the next part?
  1 = the conflict is resolved by the last line
  2 = ends on a calm or closing beat; little reason to continue
  3 = ends with a question in the air, but low stakes
  4 = ends on a clear open question, threat or decision
  5 = ends at the peak: a reveal, an attack or an ultimatum, cut before the answer

standalone: could a stranger follow who is who and what is at stake?
  1 = confusing without the earlier episodes: unclear people, unclear stakes
  2 = the main conflict is guessable but key people or stakes are unclear
  3 = followable; one or two references need context

  5 = completely self-contained: roles, conflict and stakes all clear in seconds
